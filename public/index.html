About our club

A century of focus.
A place to belong.

Target shooting is about far more than hitting the centre. It's about patience, steady technique and improving at your own pace.

Feltham & District Rifle Club is an indoor target shooting club in West London, with facilities for airgun and small-bore shooting. We're a local sporting community, united by an interest in accuracy, concentration and responsible participation.

We welcome enquiries from people interested in finding out more about the sport and the club's membership arrangements.

Concentration, consistency and the satisfaction of getting better.

Good to know

A few quick answers.

Not sure where to start? Here are some common questions.

I'm new to target shooting. Can I enquire?

Absolutely. If you're interested in learning more, contact the club about current membership and introductory arrangements. They'll be able to explain what's available.

Can I just turn up at the range?

Please contact the club before visiting. Session times, guest arrangements and access need to be confirmed.

What types of target shooting does the club offer?

The club offers air rifle and small-bore rifle target shooting. You can ask which sessions and activities are currently available when you enquire.

How do I find out about membership fees?

Get in touch with the club for current membership options, eligibility and costs. These have deliberately not been published here until confirmed.
